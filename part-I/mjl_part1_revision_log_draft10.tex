\section*{Changes}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Klose's work summarized in the literature review}
(\S11, paragraph ``Central extensions of division algebras''), at
David's request, with emphasis on \emph{Metaplektische Erweiterungen
von Quaternionenschiefk\"orpern} (Math.\ Z.\ 193).  The new text
covers: the dissertation (G\"ottingen 1983, advisor S.~J.~Patterson)
and the two papers drawn from it; the subject of the local paper
(Math.\ Z.\ 191); the global paper (Math.\ Z.\ 193): explicit cocycles
and partial results on topological central extensions of the adele
group of $\mathrm{GL}_{1,D}$ for a quaternion division algebra $D$;
the facts on commutators and symmetric cocycles attributed to Klose by
Hill, including $H^2_{\mathrm{sym}}(\mu_m,\mu_m)\cong\mathbb Z/m$; the
use of Klose's result in [J] to choose a quaternion $D$ for which the
cover of $G_D(\mathbb A_K)$ exists without $(n,r)=1$; and the
difference from the construction of the cover in [JC].  The old
sentence ``we have not compared it with Klose's construction'' is
replaced by an explicit statement of what was and was not consulted.

\emph{Sources and their limits.}  We could not obtain the text of
either Klose paper or of the dissertation: the Springer page and the
G\"ottingen digitization (GDZ) were not reachable from this session.
The summary therefore rests on:
\begin{itemize}
\item the EuDML records: Math.\ Z.\ 193, keywords ``central extensions;
quaternion algebra; constructions of cocycles'', MSC 20G10, 20G30,
11R52, 16Kxx; Math.\ Z.\ 191, keywords ``commutators; two-cohomology
class; central extensions; quaternionic skew field; metaplectic
extensions; structure of; one-units'', MSC 11S45, 16U60, 16Exx, 16Kxx;
\item Prasad--Rapinchuk, Publ.\ IH\'ES 84 (1996), Introduction (read on
numdam), which describes Klose's results as partial results on
topological central extensions of the adele group associated with
$\mathrm{GL}_{1,D}$ for $D$ a quaternion division algebra;
\item R.~Hill, arXiv:0708.0108, \S2.3 (``Commutators and symmetric
cocycles''), which says its facts were used in its reference [16] to
determine the metaplectic extensions of $D^\times$ for a quaternion
algebra over a global field, and \S3.8, which calls the isomorphism
$H^2_{\mathrm{sym}}(\mu_m,\mu_m)\cong\mathbb Z/m$ ``Klose's
isomorphism''.  We could not see Hill's bibliography, so the
identification of his [16] with one particular Klose paper is not
confirmed; the review attributes the facts to Klose, as Hill does,
without naming which paper;
\item David Joyner's 1988 paper [J], \S2.6, as posted on
yetanothermathblog.com (it cites Klose's dissertation);
\item Springer's article page (pages 625--649, received October 8,
1985; it cites [KP1] and Klose's Math.\ Z.\ 191 paper) and the
Mathematics Genealogy Project (dissertation title and advisor).
\end{itemize}
The review says in the text that the summary rests on these and that
Klose's theorem statements are not reproduced.  If you can obtain the
Math.\ Z.\ 193 paper (or the dissertation), a closer summary, and a
comparison with the cover of [JC] for $r=2$, would be worth making.

\item \textbf{Bibliography.}  [Klo86b] pages corrected to 625--649,
as on the publisher's page (EuDML and GDZ list 625--650;
Prasad--Rapinchuk also give 625--649).  New entry [Hil] (R.~Hill, arXiv:0708.0108; author and
title checked on arXiv).

\item Title line: Draft~10.  Date unchanged (November 8, 2026).
\end{enumerate}
