\section*{Scope}
Editorial changes requested by David Joyner.  \textbf{No mathematical
change}: no statement, proof, hypothesis or status label was changed.
Blocks of text were moved; the only wording changes are those listed
below.  A sorted-line comparison of Drafts~10 and~11 confirms this.

\section*{Changes requested}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Appendix A moved into Section 4} (Orbital integrals and
matching), as its last subsection, now \S6.6 ``Orbital integrals on an
elliptic torus''.  Its lemmas A.1--A.5 are now Lemmas~6.8--6.12.
\item \textbf{Appendix B moved into Section 5} (Global covers and
rational lifts), as its last subsection, now \S7.4 ``Cusp forms on
finite central covers''.  Lemma~B.1 is now Lemma~7.8.  (It supplies
Lemma~8.4(a), formerly Lemma~6.4(a), in the next section.)
\item \textbf{Literature review and notation moved to the beginning},
directly after the Introduction: \S2 Literature, \S3 Notation and
glossary.  (The section numbers~4 and~5 in the request are Draft~10's, in which
Appendix~A's material is used in \S4 and the global covers are in
\S5.)
\item \textbf{Summary of results moved to the end}, as the numbered
\S12, after the Conclusion (\S11).
\item \textbf{``Notation and conventions'' renamed ``Notation and
glossary''.}
\end{enumerate}
The paper no longer has appendices.

\section*{Wording changes forced by the moves}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item ``Appendix~A/B'' and ``App.~A'' became section references
(\S6.6, \S7.4) throughout, and ``This appendix'' became ``This
subsection'' in the two moved lead-ins.
\item The lead-ins of \S6 and \S7 now mention their new last
subsections.
\item \S1: the lead-in points to the Summary in \S12 ``at the end of the
paper'' instead of ``after this section''; \S1.6 (Organization) was
rewritten for the new order.
\item The dependency diagram in \S1.5 used hand-typed section numbers;
they are now \verb|\ref|s, so they follow the new numbering.
\item A search for other hand-typed references to Part~I's own numbers
found none (the only hit was [JC, Remark~10.3]).
\item Title line: Draft~11.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Shared glossary}
\texttt{glossary.tex}: its pointers into Part~I were renumbered for
Draft~11 (for example ``Part~I, Proposition~2.3'' is now
``Proposition~4.3''), and its header says that Part~I pointers refer to
Draft~11.  Pointers into Parts~II--IV are unchanged.

\section*{Effect on the other papers (not yet acted on)}
Equation numbers are unchanged, but 91 section, subsection and
theorem-like numbers moved (table below).  Parts~II--IV, the companion
paper and \texttt{notes/mjl\_running\_example.tex} cite Part~I by
number, using Draft~8 numbering (identical to Drafts~9--10).  In
particular, Part~II, Draft~9 (just completed) cites Part~I as
``Draft~10, whose numbering is that of Draft~8'', and its \S8 inputs
\texttt{glossary.tex}, which now uses Draft~11 numbering; if Part~II,
Draft~9 is recompiled from the repository, its glossary pointers and
its own pointers to Part~I will therefore refer to different drafts.
Recommended: a Part~II Draft~10 that renumbers its Part~I references
by this table (and renames its \S8 ``Notation and glossary'' for
consistency), and the same renumbering in the Part~III and~IV
exposition drafts and in the companion paper when it is next edited.

\section*{Old and new numbers}
Equations: unchanged.  Theorem-like environments keep their numbers
within a section, with the section number shifted by $+2$ for Draft~10
Sections~2--9.
