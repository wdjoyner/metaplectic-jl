\section*{Scope}
Part~I was reordered in its Draft~11 (literature and notation moved to
\S\S2--3, Appendices~A and~B moved into \S\S6 and~7, Summary moved to
the end); its revision log tabulates the old and new numbers.  This
draft updates Part~II's references to Part~I accordingly, at David
Joyner's request.  \textbf{No mathematical change}, and no change to
Part~II's own numbering (checked by comparing the \texttt{.aux} label
tables of Drafts~9 and~10).

\section*{Changes}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Every reference to a numbered item of Part~I now uses Draft~11
numbering: 96 occurrences of 50 distinct references (table below).
They were found in three ways: spans following ``Part~I'' (renumbered
by script from the Draft~11 table); the places that cite Part~I
without the prefix, which were edited by hand (the comparison table
of \S1.3, the dependency diagram of \S1.4, the restatements
(I1)--(I11) of \S2.3, and two sentences in \S6.2--\S6.3); and a final
search for any remaining hand-typed number, whose hits were all
external citations or Part~II's own sections.  Every change was
reviewed in a line-by-line diff.  References to Part~I, \S1 (for
example \S1.3, \S1.4, Assumption~1.1) are unchanged, as in Part~I.
\item Statements of which draft is cited: ``Draft~10 of Part~I, whose
numbering is that of Draft~8'' is now ``Draft~11 of Part~I'' (\S1
lead-in, \S2.3), and [P1] cites Draft~11.
\item \S8 renamed ``Notation and glossary'', as in Part~I; its lead-in
points to Part~I, \S3, for the shared glossary.
\item Title line: Draft~10.
\end{enumerate}
The shared \texttt{glossary.tex} was already renumbered for Part~I,
Draft~11; Part~II's text and its glossary now cite the same draft of
Part~I.
