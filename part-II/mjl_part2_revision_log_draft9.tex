\section*{Scope}
The second of the four exposition passes requested by David Joyner
(Part~I: Drafts~9--10).  \textbf{No mathematical change}: no statement,
proof, hypothesis or status label was added, removed or altered.  A
\texttt{diff} of Drafts~8 and~9 shows changes only in the title line,
in section and subsection lead-ins, in the pointers of \S\S1, 1.5, 1.6
and 2.3, in the old \S8 (Literature), which was replaced, and in the
entry [P1].

\paragraph{Numbering.}  Other Parts cite Part~II by number (Part~I and
the glossary cite Definitions~2.1 and~6.4, Remark~5.6,
Corollary~6.11, \S6.3 and \S7(iii)); none cites \S8.  The Summary of
results is therefore an unnumbered section after \S1, and the old \S8
is replaced by \S8 (Notation and conventions) and \S9 (Related work),
in the same order as Part~I.  Comparing the \texttt{.aux} label tables
of Drafts~8 and~9: every number in \S\S1--7, including theorems and
equations, is unchanged.

\section*{Changes}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{(a) Glossary.}  New \S8: a lead-in,
\verb|\input{../glossary}| (the shared file, unchanged), and \S8.11
``Notation specific to Part~II'' (seventeen entries with pointers:
local notation, $\Phi^z$, $d(\St)$ and $\lambda_v$, (I1)--(I11),
$\Omega_M$, $V_M$, $\delta_n$, $B$, $M_0$, $M_1$, $g^{1/n}$, $\sg$,

(G1), (G2), $u$-admissible pairs, $(\mathrm H_D')$, $(\mathrm H')$).
Part~II must now be compiled from \texttt{part-II/}.

\item \textbf{(b) Summary of results.}  New unnumbered section after
\S1, under one page: ten items with labels and pointers (compatible
measures; Theorem~D; germs at central good elements; orbital integrals
of arbitrary functions; linear inputs and the Steinberg-type pair;
Theorem~E; the required germ; Theorem~C$'$ and its converse; failure
of $(\mathrm H_D')$; what is left), and one line on the running
example.  All labels are those already in the paper.

\item \textbf{(c) Related work.}  The old \S8 (``Literature \ldots\ It is
preliminary'') is replaced by \S9 ``Related work'': it refers to the
review of the whole series in Part~I, \S11, and adds only what is
specific to Part~II, in two paragraphs: the inputs on covers (germ
expansions [KP2], [FK, \S8]; Zou; W.-W.~Li) and on the linear groups
(Shalika germs, Howe, Harish-Chandra, Rogawski, van Dijk,
Kivinen--Tsai, pseudo-coefficients after Kazhdan, the linear
Jacquet--Langlands inputs, Plancherel formula and formal degrees,
Kottwitz's measures, Zelevinsky's classification); and the role of the
section $\sg$.  The three sentences of the old \S8 are kept.
\textbf{No references were added}; every work mentioned was already in
the bibliography.  The roles assigned to them were checked against
their uses in the body (for example [Zel80] for the classification of
tempered representations, [Wal03] for the Plancherel formula and
Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem, [KT, Proposition~2.39] for
parabolic descent).  [BZ], [Ber] and [vD72] are in the bibliography
but not cited in the body; the new text mentions them as background
only.  A first version cited [KP1] for splitting over small subgroups;
since [KP1] is not in Part~II's bibliography, the sentence cites
[FK, \S2] only.

\item \textbf{(d) Lead-ins.}  Each of \S\S1--7 now says what it
depends on and what notation it introduces.  Subsection lead-ins were
extended where notation or a hypothesis used later is introduced:
\S\S2.2, 2.3, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 5.1, 5.2, 5.4, 6.1, 6.3.  The other
subsection lead-ins are unchanged.

\item \textbf{Part~I references.}  References to Part~I now name
Draft~10, ``whose numbering is that of Draft~8'' (\S1 lead-in,
\S2.3, [P1]); no cited number changed, since Part~I's numbering is
unchanged.

\item Title line: Draft~9, November 8, 2026.  Abstract, authors and
acknowledgments unchanged.
\end{enumerate}
