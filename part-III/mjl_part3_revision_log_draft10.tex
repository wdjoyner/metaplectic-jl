\section*{Scope}
The third exposition pass requested by David Joyner, in the layout of
Part~II (his choice): Summary of results after \S1, notation and
glossary and related work at the back.  \textbf{No mathematical
change}: no statement, proof, hypothesis or label was changed.
Part~III's own numbering in \S\S1--12 is unchanged; the old \S13
(``Selected terminology and notation''), which no other paper cites,
becomes \S13 ``Notation and glossary'', and a new \S14 ``Related work''
follows it.  This is the last separate draft of Part~III: by David's
decision the four Parts and the companion paper are now being combined
into one book, and the separate drafts are frozen.

\section*{Point for David}
Proposition~7.2 (unique splitting over $H^1$ when $p\nmid n$) and
Proposition~8.1 (non-splitting when $p\mid n$) concern the same
objects as Prasad--Raghunathan's work on topological central
extensions of $\mathrm{SL}_1(D)$ and Klose's local paper.  A comparison
would be a useful check; it has not been made.
